# Changelog

All notable player-facing changes are recorded here.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-09-15

### Added

- Drag-to-aim launching with keyboard and controller equivalents.
- Eight synthwave day backdrops and a cohesive neon interface.
- Generated neon skins for blocks, ball, paddle, and game icon.
- Explicit handcrafted layouts for all 24 rooms.
- Indestructible obstacles and moving block groups in later days.

### Changed

- Rebuilt block drawing and collisions from shared canonical paths.
- Improved paddle rebound control and stabilized collision responses.
- Added room-based starting speeds and gradual in-room acceleration.
- Refined score, pause, results, settings, and campaign screens.

### Fixed

- Prevented low-angle and near-vertical ball loops.
- Prevented collision speed drift and tunneling at higher speeds.
- Kept obstacles out of score, combo, completion, and Sunshift totals.

## 1.1.0

- Added Sunshift, five brick silhouettes, and six additional rooms.

## 1.0.0

- Initial eight-day campaign release.
