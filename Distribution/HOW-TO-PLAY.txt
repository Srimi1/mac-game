BREAK THE QUIET DAYS 1.2
========================

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
macOS 14 Sonoma or later

INSTALL
1. Drag "Break the Quiet Days.app" into your Applications folder.
2. The first time only, Control-click the app and choose Open.
3. Confirm Open in the macOS dialog.

WHY THE EXTRA FIRST-LAUNCH STEP?
This free community build is ad-hoc signed but is not yet Apple-notarized.
Control-clicking and choosing Open records your approval in macOS. Future
launches work normally.

CONTROLS
Move: mouse/trackpad, A/D, or controller left stick
Aim: drag from the waiting ball, Left/Right, or controller right stick
Launch: release the drag, Space, or controller A
Pause: Escape or controller Menu
Full screen: Control-Command-F

The game runs fully offline. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or network use.

Source and support: https://github.com/Srimi1/mac-game
